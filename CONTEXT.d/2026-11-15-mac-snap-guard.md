## 2026-11-15 -- Snap checks the Screen Recording permission on macOS, and a failed capture answers nothing (planning #307)

**Problem.** Snap on a Mac closes the app with no error (#307, known bugs). The cause is not known and this change does not claim one. Two things in the code were open at 64dec172: nothing asked macOS whether the app may record the screen before a capture started (no `systemPreferences` use anywhere in `src/`), and only the rejection of `startCapture()` was caught in `captureRectangle` (`src/main/screenshot-capture.ts`). The capture library is created lazily inside the promise and its start call sat outside any guard of its own, so a throw there rejected the whole IPC call, and the module's `captureInProgress` flag was never cleared by it, so Snap would then answer nothing for the rest of the run. The global uncaught-exception handler logs and rethrows other errors (`debug-logger.ts`).

**Change.**

- `src/main/screenshot-permission.ts` (new). `screenCaptureAllowed` takes the platform, the permission read, a message dialog and an opener as parameters, so it tests with a fake permission state on any OS. On macOS, when `systemPreferences.getMediaAccessStatus('screen')` is not `granted`, Snap says so ("Snap needs permission to record your screen, and this app does not have it yet. Open System Settings, Privacy and Security, Screen Recording, switch on AI Code Conductor, then quit and reopen the app and press Snap again.") with two buttons, Open System Settings and Not now, and captures nothing. Open System Settings opens the Screen Recording pane (`x-apple.systempreferences:com.apple.preference.security?Privacy_ScreenCapture`). A `restricted` answer (a policy blocks it) gets its own sentence with an OK only, as there is nothing to open. A read that throws lets the capture try: the capture itself is guarded now. Off macOS nothing is read or shown.
- `captureRectangle` asks first, then runs the capture. Creating the capture library, registering its listeners and calling its start are each inside a guard that ends the capture with null; the whole call is in a try and finally, so an error never leaves the module's `captureInProgress` flag set. The IPC handler and the renderer are unchanged: null already means "nothing captured" there.

**Tests.** `tests/unit/main/screenshot-snap-guard.test.ts`: the permission check with a fake state (granted, denied, not decided, unknown, restricted, unreadable, a dialog that fails, the user opening and declining, off macOS), and `captureRectangle` with a stand-in capture library that throws on creation, throws at start, rejects at start, and cancels; plus that the next Snap runs after a failed or refused one.

**Not in it.** The cause of the crash. The next step is the owner's repro on the Mac (macOS version and exact beta, the Screen Recording state before and after, whether the app closes on the click or after confirming a region, any crash report, and whether the window picker also crashes). The guard can hide a crash it does not explain, so the repro stays owed. The window-capture path (`captureWindow`) already caught its own errors and is unchanged.
