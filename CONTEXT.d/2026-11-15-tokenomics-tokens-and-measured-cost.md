## 2026-11-15 -- Tokenomics token totals, a Cost or Tokens chart, and the measured cost on its Services row (2.1.3-beta.4, PR 5)

**Problem.** The Tokenomics page showed money only. The summary the worker answers carried cost for the headline figures and for each day, and no token counts, so a model with no price showed nothing at all in the headline row and the chart, and there was no way to see how much was used apart from what it would cost. The Services row for Tokenomics had the optional `measured` block from PR 3 with nothing filling it, and no process id, because the supervisor was never told its worker's.

**Change.**

- The summary query (`src/main/tokenomics/tk-db.ts` `querySummary`) sums the tokens of the same rows its costs come from: input, output, cache read and cache write, and their total. The headline figures get `lifeToDateTokens` and `last7dTokens` beside the costs of the same windows; each day of the daily series gets `tokens` and `tokensByProvider` (its total split by provider, as `byProvider` splits the cost). Token figures count every model, priced or not: a token count needs no price. They follow every filter the costs follow (config, model, provider, account, the accounts left out in Settings, and the range for the chart only; the headline figures are all time, as before). Every existing field keeps its value and meaning.
- How the rows become the summary's figures moved into a small pure module, `src/main/tokenomics/tk-summary.ts` (the headline figures, the daily series, the token sums and their SQL), so the shape is tested on plain Node. The queries keep their place in `tk-db.ts`.
- Headline row (`KpiRow.tsx`): the life-to-date and last-seven-days cards show their tokens under the cost figure, compactly (`316.0M tokens`), with input, output, cache read and cache write in the tooltip. A summary without the figures shows none, never a zero.
- Chart (`CostOverTimeChart.tsx`): a Cost or Tokens switch in its header, styled like the filter bar's switches (the same classes and colour tokens; no new colours). It starts on Cost and keeps the choice in the chart's own state while the page stays open; the store keeps no chart state, so nothing was added there. On Tokens the chart plots each day's total tokens, its title reads "Tokens over time", and the tooltips and the top label read in tokens. The top label is drawn only when something in range is above zero, on either view: the floor that keeps a range of zeros drawable is never shown as a figure. The split by provider is its own series on Tokens (`tkSeriesInRange(..., 'tokens')`): a provider whose usage has no price has tokens and no cost, so it is drawn on Tokens and not on Cost.
- Measured cost on the Tokenomics Services row. The fork now gives the worker's process id, read each time it is asked, because a utility process has its id only once it has spawned. The supervisor puts it on the row while there is a worker, and the panel shows a PID on the Tokenomics card (`COUNTERS_KEPT.tokenomics` now has `pid`). The `measured` block carries:
  - `cpuPercent`: the worker's share of one core, from Electron's `app.getAppMetrics()` for its process id (`src/main/services/process-cpu.ts`). It is worked out from the process's own CPU seconds between two samples over the wall time between them; a worker's first sample is the average since it started. Electron's own `percentCPUUsage` is over the time since any caller last asked, so it is used only where the CPU seconds are not given. The figures are asked for only when the row is read (a Services push or the panel's read), and at most once in 5 s however often that is; a read in between gets the last answer. Each worker gets its own sampler, so a restarted worker is never measured against the one before, even under a reused id. Nothing samples in the background, so a share is the average since the sample before it: the hooks service's heartbeat (each second) and the logging worker's (each 10 s) push the Services view while they run, which keeps that to about 5 to 10 s; with neither running, the row is read only when the view is opened, and a share read long after the one before is the average over all of that time.
  - `filesTouchedLastScan`: every transcript the current worker's last full pass found in its two walks (`TkWalkStats` `transcripts.files + rollouts.files`). A new worker (after a crash, or a restart from the panel) has none until its own first full pass, and a pass the replaced worker sends late is not taken, so the two figures of one block are always one worker's. Each one found is looked at for a change on every pass, which is the cost of a scan; `filesRead` counts only the ones that changed and were opened, which is near zero on a quiet machine, so it reads less true as "files touched". A full pass that reports accepted walk figures schedules the same coalesced Services push even when the event count did not change, so a panel opened before the pass finished still gets the figure.
  - `sampledAt`: when the CPU share was sampled, or, with no CPU figure, when that pass finished.
  With Tokenomics off, with no worker (never started, down between restarts, shut down), or with no figure to give, there is no measured block.

**What crosses the preload.** No channel was added, removed or renamed, and `src/preload/index.ts` is untouched; it and `src/renderer/types/electron.d.ts` take the shapes from `src/shared/types.ts`. The `tokenomics2:summary` answer gains `kpis.lifeToDateTokens`, `kpis.last7dTokens` (and the same in each `kpisByProvider` entry), and `tokens` and `tokensByProvider` on each `dailySeries` point; the shared `TkTokenTotals` and `TkDailyPoint` types are new, in `src/shared/types.ts` and the main side's `tk-types.ts`. The service-health snapshot's Tokenomics row now carries `pid` and, when there is a figure, `measured` (both fields already existed in the shared row).

**Tests.** Host-safe, on plain Node or jsdom: `tests/unit/tokenomics/tk-summary.test.ts` (the token totals, the headline figures in all and per provider, each day and its split, the SQL names), `tests/unit/services/process-cpu.test.ts` (the share between samples and since the start, at most once in 5 s, no answer kept too, a reused process id, the fallback to Electron's share, figures that cannot be a share), `tests/unit/tokenomics/tk-supervisor-measured.test.ts` (the row's process id, the measured block, the 5 s bound, a restarted worker, no block without a worker, the registry, and the service handing over the app's figures; the figures are made up and Electron is never called), `tests/unit/renderer/tokenomics-tokens.test.tsx` (the token figures in the headline row, the switch, the token series, no top label for a range of zeros), a page render case in `tests/unit/renderer/tokenomics-page-renders.test.tsx` (a provider with tokens and no cost: one line on Cost, both providers on Tokens, and the headline row given the summary's token figures), and updated cases in `tests/unit/renderer/conductor-services-panel.test.tsx` (the PID on the Tokenomics card) and `tests/unit/renderer/tokenomics-mp12.test.tsx` (the page's chart line). The new fields are required in the shared types, so the fixture summaries in `tokenomics-mp12`, `tokenomics-new-components`, `tokenomics-optional-service` and `tokenomics-page-renders` (all under `tests/unit/renderer/`) carry them, as zero; their earlier cases are unchanged. Every new case was red under a mutant of the change (24 mutants, each red). The review round added the cases for a new worker's files figure, the long gap between two CPU samples (the choice pinned: the average over the gap), the top label and the page render, each red under its mutant (for the long-gap case, a sampler that drops a sample over a minute old); the new and changed test files were type-checked with a scratch config, since `npm run typecheck` does not cover `tests/`. For the real database, `tests/unit/native/tk-db-summary.native.test.ts` has its two daily-series cases updated for the new fields and a new group for the token totals (both windows, each day and its split, and the provider, model, account, left-out accounts and range filters); the native suite runs in CI, not on the development machine.

**Not in this change.** No token figure in the per-provider legends of the headline cards (their cost split stays as it was), no token figure on the cache card, and no measured block on the other Services rows. The page and the panel were not checked on a built app here: the Mac build and the VM smoke are the release's.
