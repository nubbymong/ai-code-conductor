## 2026-11-26 -- remote: an SSH Persistent tab runs through the holder until it is ended (1.2, with 6.3)

Plan 2.1.3-beta.19, section 1.2 with 6.3 (aicc_planning#437). Base: origin/beta at cc36c7f3. Built in two steps: 1.2a against section 1.1's contract with a fake helper; 1.2b merged the frozen heads of 1.1, 1.3, 5 and 3 and runs the real helper in remote-helper-e2e. The switch stays off by default.

- **Route.** With the switch on, an SSH Persistent Claude Code tab goes through the helper when the machine's last Check read logind as not ending the user's processes at logout and the tab is not under tmux (its own last start, a restored tab with no mark, a registry tmux entry, or an open usual-path tab of its config). A tab keeps the path it started on.
- **The mark.** The saved session carries `remoteVia: 'helper'` and the machine's device id, written at the start that goes through the helper. A marked tab never takes the usual SSH path: where the helper cannot take it, nothing starts and the tab says the step.
- **The holder.** `hold --detach --until-ended` (1.1). Close, quit, update and Restart let go of it (term.detach); only End sends term.end, over a link of its own, then removes its folder. A start whose hold exits 3 asks list mode and attaches to a launched until-ended holder, launching nothing; a clock holder, or an until-ended one with nothing launched, is ended first. The host reads and the account field read run at every start, launch or attach.
- **Where it shows.** List mode, one exec per listed machine, 5 s after start-up and when the Machines page opens: each card lists its sessions with Re-attach and End, also with the switch off for machines a left-running entry or a saved mark names. A left-running entry has mux `helper` and its device id; its liveness is the list answer, not a tmux probe.
- **6.3.** The same exec (`sessions`) removes the session folders whose holder has no record and keeps a running one's, so a folder a quit left goes at the next start.
- **Decided here.** The mark is written for SSH Persistent starts only. A machine whose Check got no answer from logind keeps SSH Persistent on the usual path. A kept session runs the helper its launch named, so a Check that installs another helper leaves the earlier folder.
- **Not done, named.** pty-manager.ts is not touched. The Settings, Feature Guide, tip, changelog, ADR-030, helper-protocol.md and threat model lines are handed to the aggregate's join. An ADR-009 pass is owed before stable.
